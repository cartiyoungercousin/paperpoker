Choose Your Opponents' Difficulty
Easy

Bots play loose and passive. They rarely bet or raise without a very strong hand, mostly just check and call, and don't factor in pot odds or equity at all.

Medium

Bots stick to decent starting hands and fold the rest. They bet when they likely have the best of it, call along with draws, and bluff only occasionally.

Hard

Bots play tight and aggressive. They only enter pots with premium hands, size bets differently for value vs. draws, and apply pressure with semi-bluffs and the odd check-raise.

Expert

Bots weigh pot odds and hand ranges on every street, mix in 3-bets and well-timed bluffs, and adapt their aggression and calling frequency to a personality you can fine-tune before each hand.
