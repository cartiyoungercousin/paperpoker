What is Poker?

  • Everyone gets 2 private cards, plus 5 shared ones in the middle
  • Betting happens in rounds as more shared cards appear
  • Best 5-card hand at the end wins the pot
  • Bluffing means you don't always need the best cards to win

Rumble

Regular Texas Hold'em, except everyone's secretly armed with a power-up.

What is Rumble?

  • Regular Texas Hold'em against 5 bots, with $1,000 starting stacks for everyone
  • A 30-second shot clock on every turn
  • A Rumble is exactly 5 hands, and whoever has the most chips when it ends wins
  • Everyone is secretly dealt one random power-up at the very start of the Rumble, yours to use once, whenever you want, on your own turn
  • Nobody knows what power-up anyone else has until they actually use it, so watch the center of the table for the reveal

🔮 Coming soon: multiplayer tournaments against real players. For now it's just you against the bots.

How Tournaments Work

  • 5 rounds of 10 hands each, against 5 bots at $1,000 starting stacks a round
  • Bot difficulty escalates every round: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, and finally Master
  • Finish a round with the strictly most chips to advance. An exact tie does not advance
  • Miss 1st place in any round and the tournament ends there, with no refund of your entry fee
  • Win every round and you take home the full payout, plus exclusive cosmetic rewards
  • Local and State are open to everyone - the higher-stakes tiers unlock as your rank climbs

Experimental Bots

A handful of personality bots for solo play, not part of the ranked ladder, just for fun.

1v1 Bots

These four always play heads-up, regardless of the player count in Game Config - other settings (stack, blinds, time limit) still apply.

The Drunk (1v1)

Bets and folds with almost no regard for hand strength. Sometimes shoves way more than the pot calls for, sometimes checks back a monster for no reason at all. Pure chaos.

The Bluffer (1v1)

Bets and raises far more often than its cards justify, but not always. It shows up with the real thing often enough that assuming a bluff every time will cost you.

The Rock (1v1)

Extremely tight and passive. Folds almost everything - genuinely alarming the rare time it finally plays a hand.

The Maniac (1v1)

Bets and raises almost constantly, regardless of hand strength. Rarely folds. Pure chaos, not a calculated bluff.
